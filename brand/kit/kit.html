04Usage

Albireo brand kit v1

Do

+Show state with brackets: [ OK ], [✓], [ Web ]
+Separate with hairlines and space, square corners everywhere
+Draw imagery in glyphs, lit by the two stars
+Write short, plain sentences joined with commas and full stops
+Numbers in mono with tabular figures
+Ease out slowly: Lenis lerp 0.075, power3 and power4 out

Don't

×Pills or fully rounded chips and buttons
×Coloured accent lines under headings or beside cards
×Decorative dots, status dots or dot separators
×Em dashes in any copy
×Gold for API data or blue for web data
×Gradients, glows or drop shadows on the logo
Voice: calm, exact, verifiable. Say what is measured and how.Never claim a number you cannot prove.
