## Interfaces and invariants

- Public new-tracker endpoint: `POST /v1/events`, a strict `WebEventV1` with server-resolved tenant. Legacy `/e` remains explicitly best effort during migration.
- Domain admission returns only after `QueuePublisher.publish(envelope, signal): Promise<void>` succeeds. The broker adapter alone performs SDK I/O.
- Web delivery uses `webReceiptId(tenantId, siteId, eventId): string`; fingerprint and effects share the Umami transaction. Point delivery has a separate canonical stream/point identity.
- Collector metrics stay `/v1/metrics`; its downstream relay is private. No in-memory queue/batch processor may acknowledge ahead of managed publication.
- Workers acknowledge broker messages only after durable, idempotent DB outcomes or committed quarantine disposition. They consult deletion/suspension tombstones before effects.
- PostgreSQL transaction helpers pin one client. Runtime roles do not own tenant tables or bypass RLS. Umami uses a separate database/role/migration boundary.
- Raw immutable points precede derived rollups. Corrections replace affected per-stream contributions; they do not increment the old result again.
- The 99.99%/99.90% targets require production measurements. No local test, replica count or supplier SLA establishes those targets.

## Progress

Execution starts with steps 1–3 in the foundation plan. Later rows are implementation work, not delivered functionality. Each later executable plan must name its concrete tests/interfaces before code changes; the roadmap's boundaries remain the rollout order.
